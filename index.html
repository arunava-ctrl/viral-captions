How to edit captions

On the video: drag a caption to move it, drag a corner or the top or bottom bar to resize it, drag the left or right bar to make the text box wider or narrower, double-clicktap to edit the text. On the timeline: tap a block to select it, then drag a block to retime it, drag its edges to trim. Here: tap a word chip to edit that word: make it a key word (the big treatment in every style), fix its text or timing, add or delete words.

My looks

Text

Shorter = words snap in faster. Short captions shorten it automatically so text always has time to show.

Text behind the speaker

Cuts the speaker out of every frame so text sits behind their head. Place the text so it overlaps them. With Auto on, each caption goes behind only where the speaker overlaps it and it stays readable. Each caption can override this in the Captions tab.

Caption length

Re-splitting rebuilds caption groups from the current words and re-picks key words.

Camera

A virtual camera operator for talking-head videos. Let it work on its own (follow the face, punch in between sentences, slowly push in), or choose Manual and decide yourself exactly when it zooms and what it aims at.

Each camera move zooms in from its start to its end, then goes back to the full picture. Moves show as purple Zoom blocks under the captions on the timeline: drag them, or drag their edges.

Background

If something important disappears (a hand, a product, a mic), pick Keep and paint over it on the video. Remove paints away bits that should be gone. Red on the video shows what gets replaced. For fine edges, zoom into the video with + under it, Ctrl + scroll or a pinch. Esc stops brushing.

Extras

Trim & cut

Edit on the timeline like in Premiere Pro. V1 holds your video clips: Split at the playhead (S), or pick the Razor (C) and click a clip; select a clip and press Delete to remove it and close the gap; drag a clip's edge to trim it. Q / W trim the clip at the playhead, I / O + Extract (X) lift out a range, J K L shuttle, ↑ ↓ jump between edits. Removed parts are listed below, ✕ brings one back.

Remove pauses

Finds the silences between sentences and cuts them for a tight, fast-paced edit. Running it again replaces the pauses it cut before.
